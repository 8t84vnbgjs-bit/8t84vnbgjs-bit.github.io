How this works

Each sport runs a catalyst-style model (v2): the engine produces a fair value, but a bet only happens when there's a named, real-world reason the market price is wrong — a goalie change, an injury, a rest spot — with the engine agreeing on direction. Engine-only disagreements are never bets: walk-forward backtests across all six sports showed they carry no signal against the closing line.

Every catalyst bet is tracked toward a mandatory 50-bet review per sport. Stakes are a flat $50 paper per play.
